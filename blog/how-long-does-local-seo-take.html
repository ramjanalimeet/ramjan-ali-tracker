How long does local SEO take? A realistic timeline

A realistic month-by-month timeline for local SEO results for home-service businesses, and what changes how fast you see them.

By Ramjan Ali Akash6 min readUpdated October 1, 2026

"How long until I see results?" is the first question every contractor asks about SEO, and the honest answer is: it depends. But it does not depend on magic. It depends on a few things you can actually assess.

What affects how fast local SEO works

  • Competition. Roofing in a big metro is much harder than septic in a rural county.
  • Your starting point. A business with 150 reviews and an old domain moves faster than a brand-new one.
  • How many problems there are. Sometimes a wrong category is the main thing holding a business back. Fixing that can show results quickly.
  • Distance. Ranking near your office is faster than ranking in towns 30 miles away.
  • Consistency. Reviews, content and links built steadily every month compound over time.

A realistic timeline

Month 1: audit and foundations

Audit, keyword and competitor research, Google Business Profile fixes, technical fixes, tracking set up and a review process started. Profile fixes can sometimes show early movement in the map within weeks.

Months 2 to 3: pages and citations

Service pages and the first service area pages built or improved. Citations cleaned up. Reviews building. You should start to see better rankings near your location and more impressions in Search Console.

Months 4 to 6: momentum

Content and local links added every month. In less competitive markets, this is often when calls from search noticeably increase. In competitive metros, rankings for the biggest terms are usually still climbing.

Months 6 to 12: compounding

The work builds on itself. Reviews, links and content keep adding prominence. Competitive terms in larger markets often land in this window, and the business becomes harder for competitors to displace.

The short version: Expect early signs in 4 to 8 weeks, real momentum in 3 to 6 months, and the strongest results in 6 to 12 months, faster in smaller markets and slower in very competitive ones.

How to tell whether it is working before rankings land

Rankings are a lagging signal. These usually move first:

  • More impressions in Search Console
  • More profile views and direction requests in your Google Business Profile
  • Rankings improving for longer, specific searches
  • Better map rankings close to your office, spreading outward

Then, the measures that matter:

  • Calls and form leads from Google
  • Booked jobs from those leads
  • Revenue from those jobs

Red flags

  • Anyone guaranteeing number one rankings by a date. Nobody controls Google.
  • Reports full of rankings for terms nobody searches.
  • No access to your own profile or website.
  • Long contracts with no clear deliverables.

Is it worth the wait?

For most home-service businesses, yes. Once you rank, every call from search comes without a per-click cost, and the rankings tend to hold as long as the basics keep going.
