The shift

The AI answer is the new map pack.

Homeowners are asking AI tools the same thing they used to type into Google: "Who is the best roofer in Tampa?" or "Which HVAC company near me has good reviews?"

The answer is a short list of names. If you are not on it, the homeowner never sees you, no matter how good your website is.

AI tools build that list from what they can find and trust about local businesses: your Google Business Profile and other listings, your reviews, your website, and what other sites say about you. My job is to make sure every one of those sources tells the same clear, strong story.

How AI picks a business

Clear facts, real proof, easy answers.

There is no secret trick. AI tools recommend the businesses they can understand and trust. These are the three things I build.

01

Clear facts

The same name, address, phone, services, hours and service areas on your Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, BBB and the directories AI tools read.

02

Real proof

Steady, genuine reviews that mention the services and towns you want, plus mentions from local news, suppliers and trade associations.

03

Easy answers

Service and area pages that say plainly what you do, where, how fast and roughly what it costs, with FAQs and schema markup AI tools can read and quote.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

Is AI search replacing Google?

Not for local services. Google Maps and search still send most calls to home service businesses. But a growing share of homeowners check an AI answer first, and those answers draw on the same signals, so I work on both together.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No. AI answers change by user, location and wording, and no one controls them. I track a fixed set of real questions every month and report how often, and how accurately, you are mentioned.

How is this different from local SEO?

It is built on local SEO. On top of that I make sure the facts about your business are consistent everywhere AI tools look, your pages answer questions clearly, and the AI crawlers can actually read your site.

Which AI tools do you track?

Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot, using questions your customers really ask, like "best roofer in [city]" or "who can pump my septic tank this week".
