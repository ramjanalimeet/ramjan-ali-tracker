How I work

One specialist, not a chain of account managers.

When you hire me, you work with me. The person on the strategy call is the person doing the work and writing your report.

I focus on the work that moves revenue: the pages people land on, the profiles they check before they call, and the trust signals that tell Google and AI tools you are the right answer.

I would rather tell you plainly that SEO is not the right move for you yet than sell you a plan that will not pay for itself.
