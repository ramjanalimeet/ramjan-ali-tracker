How it works

Different trade, same three ranking factors.

Google ranks local businesses on three things: relevance to the search, distance from the searcher, and prominence, meaning how well known and trusted the business is. That is true whether you fix garage doors or treat termites.

What changes by trade is the detail: which categories to use, which services deserve their own page, when demand peaks, and what customers worry about before they call.

I learn your trade, look at who is winning in your area and why, then build the profile, pages, reviews and links to beat them.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

My trade is not listed. Can you still help?

Very likely. If your customers search Google for a service and you go to them, or they come to you locally, the same local SEO approach applies. The audit will confirm it.

Do you work with franchises?

Yes, for individual franchise locations, as long as the brand guidelines let us manage the local profile and location pages.

How long does it take?

Profile and technical fixes often show movement in 4 to 8 weeks. Competitive terms in a large metro usually take 4 to 9 months.

Do I need a big budget?

No. The fee is a fixed monthly price agreed after the audit. You do not need to spend on ads for local SEO to work.
