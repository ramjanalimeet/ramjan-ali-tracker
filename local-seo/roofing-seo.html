The problem

Storm chasers and lead sellers are taking your calls.

Roofing is one of the most competitive local searches there is. Out-of-town storm crews, lead-generation sites and national brands all fight for the same homeowners, and many of them out-spend you on ads.

The way to beat them is local trust: a Google Business Profile that is complete and active, real reviews that mention the work, and pages for each service and town you cover. That is what Google rewards in the map pack, and it is very hard for a storm chaser to fake.

I build that foundation, then keep it growing every month so you are already ranking when the next hail or hurricane season arrives.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

How long does roofing SEO take?

Profile and technical fixes often show movement in 4 to 8 weeks. Ranking in the map pack for competitive terms like "roof replacement" in a big metro usually takes 4 to 9 months of steady work.

Can you help me rank in towns where I do not have an office?

Yes, through service area pages and organic rankings. The map pack leans heavily on distance from the searcher, so towns far from your business address are won mainly through organic results rather than the map.

Do you work with storm restoration companies?

Yes, as long as you are a real local contractor with a verifiable address. I do not create fake listings or virtual offices, because Google suspends them and the business loses everything.

What do I need to provide?

Access to your Google Business Profile and website, photos from real jobs, and a few minutes a week to ask happy customers for reviews. I handle the rest.
