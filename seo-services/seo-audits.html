Why it matters

Most businesses fix the wrong things first.

Owners often spend months on blog posts when the real problem is a wrong primary category, or on a new website when the old one just needed a few pages and faster images.

An audit finds the real gaps by comparing you with the businesses that are actually winning the map pack and organic results in your area.

Then it puts the fixes in order: what will move rankings fastest, what is a quick win, and what can wait.

What the audit covers

  • Google Business Profile setup and activity
  • Map pack rankings across your area
  • Reviews: count, rating, recency and replies
  • Citations and business detail consistency
  • Technical health and speed
  • Service and area page coverage
  • Backlinks compared with competitors
  • Tracking: calls, forms and analytics

How it works

How an engagement runs.

You always know what is being worked on this month, what lands next, and what I need from you.

  1. Audit

    I review your Google Business Profile, website, citations, reviews and the three competitors beating you in the map pack. You keep the findings either way.

  2. Plan

Is the free audit really free?

Yes. It is a 30-minute review and call with no obligation. You keep the findings either way.

What is the difference between the free audit and a deep audit?

The free audit covers the biggest issues and quick wins. A deep audit is a full review of every page, link and citation, usually done at the start of a monthly plan.

Can I use the audit with another SEO or my own team?

Yes. The fix list is yours to use however you like.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, website and the main service area. Profile and Search Console access helps for a deep audit.
