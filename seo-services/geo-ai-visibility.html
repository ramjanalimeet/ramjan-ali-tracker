Why it matters

AI answers pick a few names. Be one of them.

A classic search shows ten links. An AI answer often names just three to five businesses or products, and many people act on that shortlist without clicking anything else.

AI tools build those answers from sources they trust: well-structured pages, consistent business information, reviews, and mentions on sites that already rank.

GEO is the work of becoming one of those trusted sources. It builds on good SEO rather than replacing it, so the same work also helps your Google rankings.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization is the work of getting your business mentioned and cited in answers from AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

Is GEO different from SEO?

It builds on SEO. AI tools lean on pages and sources that already rank and are trusted, so strong SEO is the foundation. GEO adds prompt tracking, entity work, answer-ready content and mentions on cited sources.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No one can. AI answers change from one question to the next. What I can do is track your visibility across many prompts and steadily raise how often you are mentioned.

How do you measure results?

I track a fixed set of buyer prompts every month across the main AI tools and report how often you are mentioned or cited, next to traffic and leads from AI referrals.
