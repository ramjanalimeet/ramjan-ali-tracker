Why it matters

One page trying to rank for everything ranks for nothing.

A common problem on contractor sites: one "Services" page lists twenty things in bullet points. Google has no single strong page to show for "water heater replacement" or "roof inspection".

On-page SEO fixes that. Each important service gets its own page, with a title and heading that match how people search, copy that answers their real questions, and clear proof you work in their area.

Good on-page work also lifts conversions. A page that answers cost, timing and trust questions gets more calls from the same traffic.

What I optimize on each page

  • Title tag and meta description
  • H1 and heading structure
  • Service copy and FAQs
  • Local signals: towns, landmarks, NAP
  • Internal links to and from the page
  • Images, alt text and file names
  • Calls to action and click-to-call
  • Schema for the page type

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

Will you rewrite my whole website?

Only the pages that matter for rankings and leads. Many pages need edits rather than full rewrites.

Do I get to approve changes?

Yes. You see and approve new copy before it goes live, especially anything about pricing, warranties or licensing.

Is keyword stuffing still a thing?

No, and it can hurt you. Modern on-page SEO is about clear, helpful pages that match what people search for.

How fast does on-page SEO work?

Changes are often reflected within a few weeks as Google recrawls the pages.
