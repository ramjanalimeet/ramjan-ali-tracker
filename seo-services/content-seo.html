Why it matters

Thin, copy-paste pages do not rank anymore.

Many local sites are full of near-identical town pages: same text, different city name. Google treats these as doorway pages, and they rarely rank for long.

Content SEO done right means fewer, better pages. Each service page answers what a buyer needs to know. Each area page includes real local detail and real jobs. Each article solves an actual problem your customers search for.

The result is a site that ranks for more searches, earns links on its own, and gives visitors a reason to call you instead of the next result.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

Do you use AI to write content?

I may use tools for research and drafts, but every page is edited by me and built on your real jobs, photos and expertise. Generic, unedited content does not rank well or convert.

How many pages per month?

It depends on your plan and market. Quality matters more than volume. Usually a few strong pages beat many weak ones.

Do I need to write anything?

No. I will ask a few questions about your services and process, and you approve pages before they go live.

Will a blog really help a plumber or roofer?

Yes, if the articles answer real customer questions and link to your service pages. A blog with random filler posts will not.
