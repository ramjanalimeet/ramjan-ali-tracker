Why it matters

Blog traffic is not pipeline.

Many SaaS companies publish lots of top-of-funnel articles that bring visitors who never sign up. Meanwhile the searches closest to a purchase, like "best tool for X", "Y alternatives" or "Z integration", go to competitors and review sites.

SaaS SEO starts at the bottom of the funnel: the pages that convert. Then it builds outward with content that earns links and feeds those pages.

How long does SaaS SEO take?

Bottom-of-funnel pages can start bringing signups within 2 to 4 months. Competitive category terms usually take 6 to 12 months.

Do you write the content?

Yes. I write or brief every page, and your team reviews product details before anything is published.

Do you work with B2B SaaS?

Yes, B2B and B2C. The strategy changes with deal size and sales cycle, but the funnel-first approach stays the same.

How do you measure results?

Signups, trials and demo requests from organic search, alongside rankings for the pages that matter.
