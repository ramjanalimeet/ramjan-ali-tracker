The Florida market

Storms, heat and year-round demand.

Florida keeps contractors busy all year. AC is not optional, hurricane season brings roofing and restoration demand, and a large share of homes outside city sewer lines rely on septic systems.

It is also a competitive market with many out-of-state companies and lead sellers. Local trust signals, real reviews and real addresses, matter a lot here.

I focus on the towns and counties you actually serve, with service area pages and profile work that show Google you are a real local business.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

You are in Bangladesh. Can you really do local SEO for my area?

Yes. Local SEO is done through your Google Business Profile, website, citations, reviews and links, all of which I manage remotely. Local knowledge comes from keyword data, competitor research, and from you and your team. Calls are booked through my calendar at times that suit you.

Should I target the whole state?

Usually not. Focus on the metros and towns you can serve well. Statewide pages rarely rank, and Google favors businesses close to the searcher in the map.

Do you help with hurricane season preparation?

Yes. I plan profile updates, posts and content ahead of the season so you are visible when demand spikes.
