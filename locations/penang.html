The Penang market

A multilingual market with global industries.

Bayan Lepas and Batu Kawan are home to one of Asia's largest electronics and semiconductor clusters, with companies such as Intel and many suppliers, engineering firms and service providers. B2B search here reaches buyers across the world.

George Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most famous food cities in Asia. Restaurants, cafes, hotels and tour operators compete hard for visitor searches made from around the region.

Penang is also a major medical tourism destination, with private hospitals and clinics serving patients from Indonesia and further afield. Across all of these industries, customers search in English, Malay and Chinese.
