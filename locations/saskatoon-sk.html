The Saskatoon market

The service centre for a resource-rich region.

Saskatoon is the business hub for central and northern Saskatchewan. Potash, uranium, agriculture and agri-food all have a major presence, and Nutrien and Cameco are both headquartered in the city. Equipment dealers, engineering firms and service providers compete for customers who may travel hours to reach them.

The University of Saskatchewan and research facilities such as the Canadian Light Source support a strong research, agricultural biotechnology and tech sector.

Growth in Martensville, Warman and new neighbourhoods like Brighton, Evergreen and Rosewood creates steady demand for builders, trades and family services, and searchers often name their neighbourhood.
