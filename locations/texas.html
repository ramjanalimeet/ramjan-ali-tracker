The Texas market

Big metros, fast suburbs, tough competition.

Texas has several of the largest metro areas in the country, and the suburbs around them keep growing. That means a lot of searches, and a lot of contractors competing for them.

Weather drives demand here. Hail and severe storms create roofing surges, long hot summers make AC repair urgent, and hard freezes can bring waves of burst pipe calls.

Winning in Texas usually means picking your battles: ranking strongly in the suburbs and towns near your base first, then expanding outward, instead of chasing a whole metro at once.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

You are in Bangladesh. Can you really do local SEO for my area?

Yes. Local SEO is done through your Google Business Profile, website, citations, reviews and links, all of which I manage remotely. Local knowledge comes from keyword data, competitor research, and from you and your team. Calls are booked through my calendar at times that suit you.

Can I rank across all of Houston or DFW?

Not from one address in the map pack, because Google weighs distance. The usual approach is to win the map near your base and use service area pages and organic rankings to reach further.

Do you work with Texas businesses outside the big metros?

Yes. Smaller markets are often faster to win, especially for septic, HVAC and roofing.
