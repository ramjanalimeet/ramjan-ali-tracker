The Tallinn market

A digital city with a multilingual market.

Estonia is known for its digital government and its startup success stories, including Skype, Wise and Bolt. The e-Residency programme means many companies registered in Estonia are run from abroad and sell internationally, so their SEO is global from day one.

Tallinn's population speaks Estonian and Russian, and English is widely used in business. Many visitors come from Finland on the ferry from Helsinki, so restaurants, shops and services in the Old Town and the port area also see Finnish searches.

Areas such as Kalamaja, Telliskivi and Rotermann have become creative and tech districts, while Lasnamäe, Mustamäe and the suburbs around Viimsi and Harku are big residential markets with their own local needs.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

You are in Dhaka. How does SEO for a Tallinn business work remotely?

Everything that moves rankings, your Google Business Profile, website, citations, reviews and links, is managed online. Tallinn is 3 hours behind Dhaka in summer and 4 in winter, which gives almost a full working day of overlap. Local knowledge comes from keyword and competitor data for Tallinn, the rankings I track across your area, and from you and your team.

Which language should my Tallinn website use?

It depends on your customers. Local services often need Estonian and Russian, while startups and e-Residency companies usually lead with English. Content in each language is written or reviewed by native speakers.

Can you help e-Residency companies?

Yes. Most e-Residency businesses sell internationally, so the work focuses on technical SEO, content and links in the markets they target.

Do Finnish visitors matter for SEO?

For Old Town restaurants, shops and attractions, often yes. Finnish-language content and a complete profile can win visitors before they board the ferry.
