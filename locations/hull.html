The Hull market

A port city powered by new industries.

Siemens Gamesa's offshore wind blade factory at Alexandra Dock and the wider Humber energy sector have brought engineering, marine and supply-chain firms to the city. Their buyers search for specialist capabilities, certifications and project experience.

Hull's year as UK City of Culture in 2017 boosted investment in the Old Town, the Fruit Market and the marina, and visitors now come for the museums, The Deep and events. Hospitality and leisure businesses compete for that traffic online.

Locally, customers often search by area: Cottingham, Hessle, Anlaby, Willerby and Beverley are distinct markets with their own competitors, while Hull itself divides into east and west.

You are in Dhaka. How does SEO for a Hull business work remotely?

Everything that moves rankings, your Google Business Profile, website, citations, reviews and links, is managed online. Hull is 5 hours behind Dhaka in summer and 6 in winter, so we can talk any time from your morning to early afternoon. Can I rank across Hull and the East Riding?

Organically, yes, with dedicated pages for the areas you serve. In map results, Google weighs distance, so your strongest coverage is close to your address.
