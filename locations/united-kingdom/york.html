The York market

Historic homes, two rivers and a rural county.

York has one of the oldest housing stocks in England. Roofing, damp and plumbing work on period homes needs specialists, and homeowners search for them carefully.

The Ouse and Foss flood regularly, bringing water damage and drainage work.

Villages across North Yorkshire are often off mains gas and drainage, with oil heating and septic tanks.
