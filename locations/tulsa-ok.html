The Tulsa market

Storms, suburbs and an energy-built economy.

Tulsa sits in one of the most active severe-weather regions in the country. Hail, wind and tornado seasons create sudden surges in roofing, siding, glass and restoration searches, and the businesses ranking before a storm hit are the ones that get the calls after it.

The city's economy grew around oil and gas, and energy, aerospace maintenance and manufacturing still employ a large share of the region. The Tulsa Remote program and downtown districts like the Blue Dome and Arts District have also attracted new residents and small businesses.

Broken Arrow, Owasso, Jenks and Bixby have grown into large markets on their own, and many searchers name their suburb rather than Tulsa when they search.
