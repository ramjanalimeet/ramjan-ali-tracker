The Regina market

A capital city with a provincial reach.

As the provincial capital, Regina has a large public sector workforce, along with Crown corporations, consultancies and professional services that work with them. Many of those buyers research suppliers carefully online before they make contact.

Agriculture and energy shape the rest of the economy. Grain handling, fertilizer production, steel pipe manufacturing and agricultural services all have a strong presence, and the city serves a wide rural area.

Regina is also home to the RCMP training academy and to fans who travel for Saskatchewan Roughriders games at Mosaic Stadium, which bring regular visitors to the city.
