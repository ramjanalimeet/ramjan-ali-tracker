The Newcastle market

A city in transition, a region with depth.

Newcastle's port remains one of the largest coal export terminals in the world, but the city has diversified into health, education, defence, engineering and renewable energy. The University of Newcastle and John Hunter Hospital are major employers.

The Hunter Valley wine region, the beaches and Port Stephens draw tourists from Sydney and beyond, so hospitality and tourism businesses compete for visitor searches as well as local ones.

Newcastle shares its name with Newcastle upon Tyne in England. Clear location signals, such as "Newcastle NSW", Australian directories and local content, help Google show the right business to the right searcher.
