# Location research: city targets

Generated by `scripts/build_locations.py` from `scripts/city_data/`. Edit the data, not this file.

## How cities were chosen

- **Search intent:** each city is targeted with service + city searches that people type when ready to book (e.g. `boiler repair milton keynes`, `ac maintenance sharjah`), matching the trades on this site.
- **Demand:** growth (new housing, census and planning data), weather and climate (hail, hurricanes, cyclones, extreme heat, cold winters) and housing stock (old homes, off-mains septic, villas) that create steady urgent searches.
- **Competition:** big metros (Houston, Dallas, London, Manchester, Toronto, Sydney, Dubai) were excluded because specialist agencies already compete there. Spot checks of these mid-size cities in October 2026 mostly turned up directories, marketplaces or templated agency pages, which genuinely local pages can outrank.
- **Tiers are estimates.** No paid keyword tool was used. Before investing heavily in a city, confirm volumes in Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or Semrush and check the live map pack.
