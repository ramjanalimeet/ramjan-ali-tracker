Local results, managed remotely.

Everything that moves local rankings, your profile, website, citations, reviews and links, is managed online. I am based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and work with US businesses remotely.

How it works

How an engagement runs.

You always know what is being worked on this month, what lands next, and what I need from you.

  1. Audit

    I review your Google Business Profile, website, citations, reviews and the three competitors beating you in the map pack. You keep the findings either way.

  2. Plan

    Target services, towns and keywords, written down as one 90-day roadmap you approve before any work starts.

  3. Fix and build

    Profile cleanup, technical fixes, service and area pages, citations, and a review system. The foundations go in first.

  4. Grow and report

    Content, links and profile activity every month, with a plain-English report on rankings, calls and form leads.
