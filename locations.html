How I work remotely

Local results, managed from anywhere.

Everything that moves rankings, your Google Business Profile, website, citations, reviews and links, is managed online. I am based in Dhaka, Bangladesh (GMT+6), and work with businesses across North America, Europe, the Gulf and Asia-Pacific.

Local knowledge comes from three places: keyword and competitor data for your exact market, the rankings I track across your service area, and you and your team, who know your customers better than anyone. For non-English markets, native speakers write or review every page.

How it works

How an engagement runs.

You always know what is being worked on this month, what lands next, and what I need from you.

  1. Audit

    I review your Google Business Profile, website, citations, reviews and the three competitors beating you in search. You keep the findings either way.

  2. Plan

    Target services, areas, languages and keywords, written down as one 90-day roadmap you approve before any work starts.

  3. Fix and build

    Profile cleanup, technical fixes, service and area pages, citations, and a review system. The foundations go in first.

  4. Grow and report

    Content, links and profile activity every month, with a plain-English report on rankings, calls and form leads.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

You are in Bangladesh. Can you really do SEO for my city?

Yes. Rankings are driven by your Google Business Profile, website, citations, reviews and links, all of which I manage remotely. Local knowledge comes from keyword and competitor data for your area, the rankings I track there, and from you and your team. Calls are booked at times that suit your time zone.

My city is not listed. Can you still help?

Yes. These are the markets I have researched in depth, but the same process works anywhere Google is the main search engine. Book a call and we will look at your market together.

Can you do SEO in languages other than English?

Yes. I plan the structure, keywords and hreflang setup for each language, and the content is written or reviewed by native speakers so it reads naturally and ranks well.

Can I rank in a city where I have no office?

In organic results, yes, with genuinely useful location pages. Map pack rankings depend heavily on distance from your verified address, so they are strongest near where you are based.
