How I work remotely

Local results, managed remotely.

Everything that moves rankings, your website, profiles, listings, reviews and links, is managed online. I am based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and work with clients across five regions.

Local knowledge comes from three places: keyword and competitor data for your exact area, rankings tracked across your service area, and you and your team, who know your market better than anyone.

How it works

How an engagement runs.

You always know what is being worked on this month, what lands next, and what I need from you.

  1. Audit

    I review your site, profiles, content, links and the competitors ranking above you. You keep the findings either way.

  2. Plan

    Target services, pages, keywords and markets, written down as one 90-day roadmap you approve before any work starts.

  3. Fix and build

    Technical fixes, page improvements and new content. The foundations go in first, so later work compounds.

  4. Grow and report

    Content, links and visibility work every month, with a plain-English report on rankings, traffic and leads.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

You are in Bangladesh. Can you really do local SEO for my area?

Yes. Local SEO is done through your Google Business Profile, website, listings, reviews and links, all of which are managed online. Rankings are tracked from inside your service area, not from my location.

Are prices different by country?

No. Prices are the same in every region and listed in USD on each service page.

Can you handle multiple countries for one business?

Yes. International sites get a clear country or language structure with hreflang, so each market sees the right pages.
