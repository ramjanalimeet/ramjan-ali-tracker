How Google ranks local businesses

Relevance, distance and prominence.

Relevance is how well your business matches the search. Your categories, services, website pages and reviews all signal what you do.

Distance is how far you are from the searcher or the location in the search. You cannot change it, but you can make the most of it with a properly set service area and local pages.

Prominence is how well known and trusted you are. Reviews, links, citations and mentions across the web all build it.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

What is the map pack?

The map pack is the box of three local businesses with a map that Google shows for searches like "plumber near me". It gets a large share of clicks and calls for local searches.

Do I need a physical address?

You need a real business address to verify a Google Business Profile, but service-area businesses can hide it and show the areas they serve instead.

How much does local SEO cost?

It is a fixed monthly price based on your market and scope, agreed in writing after the free audit.

Will you post fake reviews or create extra listings?

Never. Both break Google's rules and the FTC's rules on fake reviews, and they put your business at risk.
