Why local + AI search

The map pack gets the calls. AI answers decide who makes the list.

When a homeowner's AC dies or a pipe bursts, they search Google, look at the three businesses on the map, check the stars, and call one of them.

More and more of them now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity first, or read Google's AI Overview before they scroll. "Who is the best-reviewed plumber near me?" gets a short list of names, and everyone else is invisible.

Both lists are built from the same signals: a complete Google Business Profile, consistent listings, steady reviews, clear service and area pages, and real local mentions. I build those signals so you are the business Google ranks and AI recommends.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the work of getting a business to show up in Google Maps and local search results for searches near it, like "plumber near me" or "roof repair in Tampa". It covers your Google Business Profile, website, reviews, citations and local links.

What is AI search visibility?

It is how often, and how accurately, AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews mention your business when someone asks them for a local service. Those answers are built from your Google Business Profile, reviews, directory listings and what your website and the wider web say about you.

Do I need both?

They are one job. The signals that put you in the map pack, a complete profile, consistent listings, real reviews and clear service pages, are the same ones AI tools rely on. I work on both together in one plan.

How long does it take?

Profile and technical fixes often show movement in 4 to 8 weeks. Competitive terms in large metros usually take 4 to 9 months of consistent work. AI answers tend to follow as your profile, reviews and listings get stronger.

What does it cost?

Each plan is a fixed monthly price based on your market, competition and the number of services and areas. You get the exact price and scope in writing after the free audit, before anything starts.

Can you guarantee #1 on Google or in ChatGPT?

No honest SEO can. Google and the AI companies control their results. What I can promise is clear work, clear reporting, and a focus on calls and jobs.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans are month to month and can be paused or cancelled with a week of notice.
