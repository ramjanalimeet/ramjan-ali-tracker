Why local SEO

The map pack is where the calls are.

When a homeowner's AC dies or a pipe bursts, they search Google, look at the three businesses on the map, check the stars, and call one of them.

Google picks those three using relevance, distance and prominence. Local SEO is the work of making your business the most relevant and most trusted option near the searcher.

That means a fully built Google Business Profile, a fast website with a page for every service and area, consistent listings, steady reviews and real local links. I handle all of it.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Anything else, ask on the call or email me. I reply to every message personally.

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the work of getting a business to show up in Google Maps and local search results for searches near it, like "plumber near me" or "roof repair in Tampa". It covers your Google Business Profile, website, reviews, citations and local links.

How long does local SEO take?

Profile and technical fixes often show movement in 4 to 8 weeks. Competitive terms in large metros usually take 4 to 9 months of consistent work.

What does it cost?

Each plan is a fixed monthly price based on your market, competition and the number of services and areas. You get the exact price and scope in writing after the free audit, before anything starts.

Can you guarantee #1 on Google?

No honest SEO can. Google controls the rankings. What I can promise is clear work, clear reporting, and a focus on calls and jobs.

Do I own my Google Business Profile and website?

Yes. Everything stays in your name. I work with access you can remove anytime.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans are month to month and can be paused or cancelled with a week of notice.
