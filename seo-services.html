How to choose

Which service fits your business?

Local businesses that need calls from nearby customers should start with Local SEO. It covers your Google Business Profile, website, reviews and local links in one plan.

Online stores should look at Ecommerce SEO, or Shopify SEO if you run on Shopify. Both focus on category and product pages, where most buying searches land.

Any site that has stalled usually needs an SEO Audit first. It shows whether the problem is technical, content or authority, so you only pay for what will help.

How it works

How an engagement runs.

You always know what is being worked on this month, what lands next, and what I need from you.

  1. Audit

    I review your site, profiles, content, links and the competitors ranking above you. You keep the findings either way.

  2. Plan

    Target services, pages, keywords and markets, written down as one 90-day roadmap you approve before any work starts.

  3. Fix and build
