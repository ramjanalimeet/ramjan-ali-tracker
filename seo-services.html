Why it matters

Your website is a ranking factor for the map.

Google looks at your website when deciding how relevant and trusted your business is. The page linked from your Google Business Profile matters a lot.

A fast, well-structured site with a strong page for each service gives Google clear signals, and gives visitors a reason to call.

These services fix the website side of local SEO, so your profile and your site work together.

Signs your site is holding you back

  • One "Services" page listing everything
  • Town pages with the same text and a different city name
  • Slow loading on phones
  • Pages missing from Google's index
  • No reviews or proof on service pages
  • Hard to find the phone number
How it works

You always know what is being worked on this month, what lands next, and what I need from you.
